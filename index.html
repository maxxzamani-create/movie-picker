How it works

Plug in. It watches. It catches. It snaps back.

01 · INSTALL

One box on the HDMI line

Between your cable/streaming box and the TV. Works with almost any setup you already run — no rewiring, no IT department. Your broadcast signal is never modified.

02 · THE CATCH

It reads the broadcast itself

A commercial break and a 25-second timeout can look identical. BreakShark reads the broadcast itself to tell them apart, so it takes over only during real ad breaks — and never cuts away from live play.

03 · SNAP BACK

The game returns, so does the screen

The moment play resumes, every screen is back on the broadcast. Your crowd keeps watching; your register keeps the upside.

And the 5% we miss? Nothing happens. The break simply plays as it always has. Worst case with BreakShark is the TV you already own — every catch is pure upside.

Run it from your phone

Change your lineup anywhere — add spots, reorder, shuffle — and the box picks it up in under a minute. During a break the panel splits: the commercial being blocked, and your ad running live. Every takeover counted.

Straight answers

Five questions owners actually ask

Does it ever interrupt the live game?

No. BreakShark only changes what your screen shows during the commercial break. The game itself is never touched, delayed, or interrupted — when play resumes, so does your broadcast.

Does it interrupt the game or touch my feed?

No. Detection never modifies the broadcast signal — it only changes what your local screen displays during a break that's already happening, and snaps back when play resumes.

What do I need to get started?

Photos of your food and drinks. You build your rotation from the dashboard; founding partners get 3 professional spots produced from their photos.

I don't show sports — is this for me?

Yes. In Loop-Only mode the box runs your own promotions all day with no broadcast at all — waiting rooms, salons, lobbies, showrooms. If you ever add live TV, the same box starts catching breaks automatically.

Why is there no price on the site?

Because it isn't set yet, and we won't invent one for a landing page. Founding partners help shape pricing — inquire and we'll walk you through it.
