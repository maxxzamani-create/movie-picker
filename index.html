CHANNELS

Hands off the TVs

Tell it tonight's games. When one game ends, the box changes the channel on the receiver to the next one, on every screen it drives. Nobody hunts for the remote or climbs for the guide. If your staff change a channel by hand, the box backs off and leaves it alone for two hours.

KILL SWITCH

Your staff always wins

One tap and the box stops touching the TVs. It stands down whenever a person is driving the room — a box that fights the bartender gets unplugged.

MUSIC

The vibe never dies

No dead air in the break. When a TV's volume is up, your ads play over light background music, so the room never drops into silence and the vibe stays in check from the first pitch to the last call. A muted TV stays muted.

LIGHTS

Know it's working from across the room

The light on the front of the box tells your staff what it's doing: green, the game is on; blue, your ad is up; blinking teal, no picture is coming from the cable box, so check it; red alone, restart the box.

Your ads, made from
your own photos.

Not stock footage. Not some invented place that looks nothing like yours. Your dish, your bar, your room — the pictures already on your website — turned into moving spots that play in your breaks. You give us your web address and tick the ones you like.

  • Paste your website. About a minute later every photo on it comes back as a card, each with a line already written for it and a note saying which page it came from.

You do the fun part: pick what you want to show off, say what the spot should say, and choose six seconds or ten. We do the rest. Every spot comes out in HD, and making them is free. Make as many as you like and play them as often as you want.

Questions

The things owners actually ask.

Does this mess with my TV provider?

No. BreakShark never alters or rebroadcasts the channel. It reads the picture coming down your own line and switches which input your TVs show — the same thing a person with a remote does, done faster and automatically. It runs on DirecTV for Business today, and on cable and streaming boxes. If your provider wants it in writing, ask and we'll put what it does and doesn't touch on paper for them.

What if it ever sits out a break?

Then the break plays like it always has, the same as any night before you had us. When it is unsure it leaves the game on. Graded second by second against thousands of hours of live TV, it is 99.5% accurate on live sports, and every figure on this page traces to a graded file.

What does it cost?

Pricing is set with the Founding 10, and we'd rather say that than invent a number. Making spots in Ad Studio is free, and everything in your library plays as often as you want.

What happens to the sound?

Most bar TVs run muted, and a muted TV stays muted. If a TV's volume is up, your ads play over light background music during the break, so the room never drops into dead air.

Can my staff override it?

Always. There's a kill switch, and the box stands down whenever someone is driving the TVs by hand. It's built to lose that argument on purpose.

What about slow afternoons with no game on?

Loop mode runs your reel on a schedule with no live TV involved, so the screens still sell for you at two in the afternoon. And if there’s nothing on worth watching, don’t worry: we have funny, interesting, captivating videos that play whenever nothing else is on. They keep the room watching, so your offers keep landing in front of them. How loop mode works →

I've got 14 TVs. Do I need 14 boxes?

No — screens are never what gets counted. One SBX-1 sits on one cable or streaming box and covers every TV showing that feed. What you count is simultaneous games: three different games at once means three feeds, so three boxes. Tell us how many games you run at once on a Saturday and we'll give you the count before we come out. Restaurant groups: same text, we'll scope the whole group.
