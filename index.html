Straight answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Does this interrupt the game or the live broadcast?

No. AdShark only takes over during the commercial break that's already happening — it snaps back to the live broadcast the instant programming resumes. Nobody misses a play, and the broadcast signal itself is never modified.

Do I need new TVs or complicated hardware?

No. AdShark is compatible with almost every TV and streaming setup already running in your bar. The Starter plan includes a one-time $199 AdShark box; Full Bar includes the boxes for every screen as part of the 12-month term.

What do I actually need to provide to get started?

Photos of your food and drinks. On Starter, you build your own rotation of up to 3 promo spots. On Full Bar, our team produces 3 professional spots from your photos at no extra charge.

How do I know it's actually working?

Every takeover is logged on your owner dashboard — play counts, timing, and an estimate of what each play was worth, so you can watch the leak turn into revenue instead of just taking our word for it.

Is this legal — does it mess with my cable or streaming feed?

AdShark's detection never modifies the broadcast signal. It only changes what your local screen displays, and only during a commercial break that's already playing. Your cable box, streaming device, and the underlying feed are untouched.

What's the "10 founding spots" offer?

We're taking ten founding restaurants and bars through Phase 2 of the rollout — first month free, with your price locked for life as an early partner. After that, pricing moves to the standard tiers above.
