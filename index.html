How it works

DETECT. TAKE OVER. SNAP BACK.

Notices the break

AD-SHARK watches the broadcast right along with your customers and knows the instant it cuts to commercials — no waiting, no lag.

Puts your menu on screen

Your own promo spots play instead: real ads for your food, built from your photos, each getting equal airtime. Your customers stay your customers.

Snaps back to the game

The instant programming resumes, the game is back on. Nobody misses a play — you never lose the broadcast, you just get the commercial breaks back.

Shows you the payoff

Your dashboard logs every play and every second your screens spent selling for you, so you can watch it turn into bigger checks.

📺 Works with what you already have. AD-SHARK is compatible with almost every TV and streaming setup on the market today — no new hardware headaches, no IT department, no changing how your bar already runs.
