Short false alarms

Satellite · the weak spot

Satellite is harder and we're not there yet. On a DirecTV baseball test: 2 breaks caught clean, 1 caught late, 1 missed. Satellite ads splice in with no gap for the box to see. Streaming is proven; satellite is still being worked on.

HOW FAST IT FLIPS: on the frame-by-frame test, your ad was up about 2 seconds into the break — sometimes before it even started — and never later than 12 seconds. Coming back, it's typically on the game about 5 seconds early, during the channel's bumper. It has never cut into live play.
And if it ever fails? Nothing happens.

The break just plays like it always has — the game feed runs straight through the box. Lose power, unplug it, whatever: your TV is the TV you already own. And the bypass remote in your drawer shuts it off instantly — no menu, no calling us. Every catch is money you didn't have; a miss costs you nothing.

Straight answers

What owners actually ask

Does it ever interrupt the game?

No. Your broadcast is never changed, delayed, or interrupted. The BX-1 only changes what your screen shows during a commercial break that's already happening — and the game is back the moment play resumes. In the frame-by-frame test it actually came back about 5 seconds early, during the channel's bumper.

What happens when it misses one?

Nothing. The break plays like it always has — the game runs straight through the box. On streaming it's caught 62 out of 62 in testing. On satellite it's caught 2 of 4, and we say so right on the page. Worst case is the TV you already own.

I've got 14 TVs. Do I need 14 boxes?

One box covers one cable or streaming box. If six TVs are all showing the same cable box, one covers all six. Rooms with a bunch of different feeds are exactly what a Catch Night is for — we walk your setup and give you the honest count before anything else happens.

What do you need from me to start?

Photos of your food, your drinks, and your room. Founding venues get 3 professional spots made from those photos, and you run the lineup from your phone.

Why isn't there a price on the site?

Because it isn't set, and we're not going to invent one for a webpage. How it works is set: you buy the box, one flat fee, you keep everything. The first 10 venues help set the number. Nothing gets billed until you've agreed to it.
