Straight answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Does this interrupt the game or the live broadcast?

No. BreakShark only takes over during the commercial break that's already happening — it snaps back to the live broadcast the instant programming resumes. Nobody misses a play, and the broadcast signal itself is never modified.

Do I need new TVs or complicated hardware?

No. BreakShark is compatible with almost every TV and streaming setup already running in your bar. The Starter plan includes a one-time $199 BreakShark box; Full Bar includes the boxes for every screen as part of the 12-month term.

What do I actually need to provide to get started?

Photos of your food and drinks. On Starter, you build your own rotation of up to 3 promo spots. On Full Bar, our team produces 3 professional spots from your photos at no extra charge.

How do I know it's actually working?

Every takeover is logged on your owner dashboard — play counts, timing, and an estimate of what each play was worth, so you can watch the leak turn into revenue instead of just taking our word for it.

Is this legal — does it mess with my cable or streaming feed?

BreakShark's detection never modifies the broadcast signal. It only changes what your local screen displays, and only during a commercial break that's already playing. Your cable box, streaming device, and the underlying feed are untouched.

What about those "free" TV boxes that pay me to run ads?

Those companies sell your commercial-break time to their advertisers — national beverage brands, sportsbooks — and mail you a cut, typically $1–2K a year. Your own specials share the break with ads you didn't choose. BreakShark flips the model: every second of every break sells your menu, and you keep 100% of the upside. Their own published case study showed venue self-promos lift food & drink sales 6.5% — worth about $65K a year in a $1M room. We think you should keep that, not rent it out for $1,500.

What's the "10 founding spots" offer?

We're taking ten founding restaurants and bars through Phase 2 of the rollout. Founding partners get their first month free and today's standard pricing locked in — plus priority installation, hands-on onboarding from the founder, and first say in what we build next.
