QUICK ANSWERS

Will my customers miss any of the game?

No. AdShark only takes over during commercial breaks and snaps back the instant programming resumes. The broadcast itself is never modified — we just change what the screen shows during the break, exactly like a bartender flipping inputs, but automatic.

Does it work with my TV setup?

AdShark works with practically every TV and standard cable, satellite, or streaming setup. The box installs inline in about an hour with no rewiring. If your setup is unusual, that's exactly what the founding pilot is for — tell us in the form.
