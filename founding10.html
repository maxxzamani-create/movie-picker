QUICK ANSWERS

Will my customers miss any of the game?

No. BreakShark only takes over during commercial breaks and snaps back the instant programming resumes. The broadcast itself is never modified — we just change what the screen shows during the break, exactly like a bartender flipping inputs, but automatic.

Does it work with my TV setup?

BreakShark works with practically every TV and standard cable, satellite, or streaming setup. The box installs inline in about an hour with no rewiring. If your setup is unusual, that's exactly what the founding pilot is for — tell us in the form.

What does it cost?

Pricing has not been set yet — founding restaurants get up to 3 months free and help shape it. What we can promise: it will be priced so competitively that one extra sale a day covers it. Claim a spot and we'll walk you through it on the call — no card, no commitment.

Can I sell my ad slots to other local businesses?

Yes — ask about Partner Ads, available on our premium plans. Your screens, your choice: sell rotation slots to neighboring businesses — the mechanic, the barber, the bar down the street — as many as you want, and charge them whatever you want. You keep 100% of it; we never broker it, never take a cut, never interfere. Many venues can cover their entire BreakShark cost this way before their own sales lift even counts.

What's the catch?

You're a pilot partner: we ask for honest feedback and your real sales numbers so we can prove (or disprove!) the lift. If it doesn't earn its keep, cancel — we'll take the box back, no hard feelings.
