How it actually goes

Catch Night. Install.
Then your own numbers.

Three steps, in this order. Nobody skips the first one.

01 · CATCH NIGHT FIRST

Free, nothing to sign

I bring the BX-1 to your place and run it on your TVs for one real game night. You hold the kill switch the whole time. If you shrug, the box goes home with me and that's the end of it.

02 · INSTALL

About an hour, before open

The box goes between your cable or streaming box and the TV. No rewiring, no IT. Your game feed is never changed or delayed, and the bypass remote lives in your drawer.

03 · YOUR RESULTS PAGE

Your numbers, in public

You get your own page showing what the box did in your room — breaks caught, promos run, and what your app and dessert sales did. Good or bad, it's the number that goes up.

IF THE BOX EVER FAILS, NOTHING HAPPENS. The break just plays normally — the game feed runs straight through. And the bypass remote in your drawer kills it instantly, no menu, no phone call.
