TRADE-SECRET ENGINE

The detection tuning and pattern library stay proprietary — and get smarter with every venue deployed. The data moat compounds; a copycat starts from zero.

FAIL-SAFE BY PHYSICS

The broadcast never passes through our computer. If our software ever hiccups, the TV shows the game. Worst case is always "nothing happens" — that's what lets bars trust a startup's box.

CLEAN LEGAL POSTURE

We never record, store, modify, or redistribute the feed. Switching inputs is what a bartender does with a remote — the venue owns the screen and decides what it shows. Production design uses only licensing-clean detection paths.

FIRST-MOVER IN THE WEDGE

Locked-in founding cohort, the adshark.tv brand, and speed. The realistic endgame — partnership or acquisition by a strategic already on venue screens — favors whoever proves the category first.

CAPITAL-EFFICIENT BY RULE

Standing rule: no production spend until the founding 10 are paying. Pilot on retail parts, prove retention, then scale. Break-even at ~1% of the niche keeps every path open.
