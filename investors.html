THEY MONETIZE THE BREAKS. WE HAND THEM BACK.

Taiv spent years and $13M+ proving the two things we'd otherwise have to prove: bars install boxes to control commercial breaks, and venue self-promotion measurably lifts sales — their own flagship case study is a pub group gaining +6.5% food & drink sales from its OWN promos. Then they structurally capped how much of that value a venue can keep (1 of 6 slots free; pay up for more; 2-year post-exit non-compete; house promos contractually "best-effort"). BreakShark sells the uncapped version: venue-paid, every slot theirs, no ad network to protect. The incumbent validated the behavior; their business model forbids them from selling the pure product.

THE MOAT

PATENT STRATEGY

U.S. patent pending — Provisional Application No. 64/114,099, filed July 17, 2026. 45 claims covering the automated break-detection and display-takeover system, multiple detection sources, and the networked marketplace embodiment. Nonprovisional conversion planned within the priority year.

TRADE-SECRET ENGINE

The detection tuning and pattern library stay proprietary — and get smarter with every venue deployed. The data moat compounds; a copycat starts from zero.

FAIL-SAFE BY PHYSICS

The broadcast never passes through our computer. If our software ever hiccups, the TV shows the game. Worst case is always "nothing happens" — that's what lets bars trust a startup's box.

CLEAN LEGAL POSTURE

We never record, store, modify, or redistribute the feed. Switching inputs is what a bartender does with a remote — the venue owns the screen and decides what it shows. Production design uses only licensing-clean detection paths.

ALIGNED-MODEL MOAT

The incumbent (Taiv) makes money selling the exact seconds we liberate — matching our offer for every corner bar means cannibalizing their own ad network. Our moat isn't the feature; it's a business model an ad network can't copy without firing its real customer. Plus: founding cohort, brand, and speed toward the realistic endgame — partnership or acquisition by a strategic already on venue screens.

CAPITAL-EFFICIENT BY RULE

Standing rule: no production spend until the founding 10 are paying. Pilot on retail parts, prove retention, then scale. Reaching self-sustaining at under 1% of the niche keeps every path open.
